About LnJ Home Services

Built on Reliability & Workmanship Integrity

Established in May 2025 and originally founded by a university student as an independent local business, LnJ Home Services proudly serves homeowners, landlords, and businesses throughout the Durham Region.

We specialize in residential and light commercial renovations, flooring, landscaping, equipment assembly, and general property maintenance. By combining hands-on practical experience with clear communication and fair pricing, we make sure every task—big or small—is completed safely, efficiently, and to the highest standard.

Our Mission

To deliver consistent, high-quality craftsmanship on every single project—completing each job on schedule, safely, and right the first time.

Our Vision

To be Durham Region's most dependable, approachable partner for cost-effective home repairs, renovations, and outdoor improvements.
